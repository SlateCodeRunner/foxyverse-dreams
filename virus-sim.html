How it works: Cells bounce around like Algodoo particles. When an infected cell (red) gets within the infection radius of a healthy cell (green), there's a chance of transmission. Infected cells either recover (blue) or die (gray) after a set time. Use tools to interact: infect, heal, add walls, vaccinate. The graph tracks the SIR curve in real time. Drag on the canvas to draw quarantine walls.
